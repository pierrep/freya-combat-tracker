Getting recordings in:

data/sounds/README.md explains the file names (for example wood/d20_face_hard_2.wav), which clip a hit falls back to, and how to record your own.

tools/split_dice_hits.py cuts a long recording into one clip per hit. 
It sorts them into soft, medium and hard by loudness and names them. 
Use --list to see what it finds before writing anything, and --credit to add a credit line for CC-BY recordings. 
It reads WAV directly; MP3, FLAC and M4A need ffmpeg. 

Run it as python3 tools/split_dice_hits.py …, since it may not be marked executable on your machine.

Recordings at the right level play about as loud as the synthesiser does for the same hit.
